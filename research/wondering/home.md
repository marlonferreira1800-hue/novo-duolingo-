How to Apply Stoic Philosophy

Stoicism offers a practical toolkit for remaining calm and resilient in a chaotic world — far from the cold detachment most people imagine.

Stoicism vs. Epicureanism

StoicismEpicureanism

Stoicism

- Virtue is the only good
- Endurance of hardship
- Focus on social duty

Epicureanism

- Absence of pain is good
- Avoidance of stress
- Focus on simple pleasures

The Three Stoic Disciplines

The Three Stoic Disciplines

| Discipline | Focus Area | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desire | Values & Passions | Wanting only what is in our control |
| Action | Social Duty | Acting with justice and for the common good |
| Assent | Judgment | Avoiding errors in how we perceive events |
